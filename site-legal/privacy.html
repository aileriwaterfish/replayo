Replayo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

1. Single-user application

Replayo is a personal desktop application used exclusively by its author. It has no other users, no accounts, and no user database.

2. Data collected

Replayo does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. All recordings (screen captures, game clips, edited videos) are created and stored locally on the author's own computer.

3. TikTok data

When the TikTok integration is used, Replayo stores the OAuth tokens for the author's own TikTok account locally on the author's computer. These tokens are used solely to upload the author's own videos as drafts to the author's own account, via TikTok's official Content Posting API. No data about any other person is accessed, collected, or processed.

4. Third-party services

The only external service Replayo communicates with is TikTok's official API, and locally the League of Legends client APIs on the author's machine (127.0.0.1). No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
