Replayo — Terms of Service

Last updated: July 29, 2026

1. What Replayo is

Replayo is a personal desktop application for Windows, developed and used by a single individual (the author). It continuously records the author's own screen, detects highlights in the author's own gameplay sessions, and edits short highlight videos.

2. Scope of use

Replayo is not distributed, sold, or offered as a service to third parties. The only user of the application is its author. All recorded content is the author's own gameplay.

3. TikTok integration

Replayo can upload the author's own edited videos as drafts to the author's own TikTok account, using TikTok's official Content Posting API. Nothing is published automatically: each draft is manually reviewed in the TikTok mobile app before any publication decision.

4. Liability

The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. The author is solely responsible for the content they record and choose to publish.
